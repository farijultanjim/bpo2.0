[DIV] Frequently asked questions Straightforward answers on transition, savings, security and scale.
[DIV] 01 How does the transition work, and how long does it take?
[DIV] Every step is planned and signed off with you: an intro call on your processes, volumes and KPIs, an NDA, two shadow sessions where our team watches your process live, SOP creation, two pilot runs under your review, a master SOP you approve, then go-live with KPI tracking. Timing depends on the process, but most teams are live within a few weeks.
[DIV] 02 How much does an offshore FTE actually save us?
[DIV] An offshore FTE costs around $1,500 a month, or $18,000 a year, against a US average salary of $65,000. That is $47,000 saved per person per year in salary alone, before bonuses, healthcare, 401K, overhead and equipment. Your team member works full US hours and performs the same tasks as a US-based employee.
[DIV] 03 Do you work inside our systems, or do we have to change our tools?
[DIV] We work inside your own systems: your TMS, ERP, carrier portals, email and CRM. Nothing moves out of your environment, and the SOPs we write document your process as it already runs, so there is no re-platforming before go-live.
[DIV] 04 How is our data protected?
[DIV] An NDA is signed before any shadow session, and all work runs under an information security management system certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022. The certification is issued by an accredited certification body.
[DIV] 05 Can the team scale with us, including after hours?
[DIV] Yes. With 500+ trained team members across Dubai, New York, Dhaka and Singapore, we add FTEs based on your expansion plans or volume surges. Teams work in multiple shifts, so you get 24/7 operations support in real time rather than a next-morning catch-up.
[DIV] 06 How do we stay in control once the team is live?
[DIV] You set the KPIs and milestones, and we report against them. Go-live comes with KPI tracking, strong governance and regular client connects, and the master SOP stays yours, so the know-how never leaves your business.
